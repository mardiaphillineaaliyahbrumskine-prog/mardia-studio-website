About MARDIA STUDIO

MARDIA STUDIO brings AI-assisted filmmaking tools into one approachable creative environment. The vision is simple: make the filmmaking process easier to explore for creators who have stories to tell, without requiring professional prompting or complicated production workflows.

From Idea to Screen

MARDIA STUDIO is designed around a creator-friendly workflow that keeps the technology powerful while keeping the experience simple.

01 — Story

Develop an idea into a structured story and screenplay concept with AI assistance.

02 — Characters

Explore character concepts, appearances, voices and creative identities.

03 — Scenes

Turn visual ideas into cinematic scene concepts and AI-assisted video experiences.

04 — Voice

Develop dialogue and voice experiences to support the story.

05 — Edit

06 — Experience

Preview the developing film and bring the different creative elements together.
